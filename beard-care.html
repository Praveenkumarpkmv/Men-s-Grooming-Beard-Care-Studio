Beard Care · Apothecary

Treat your beard like it's got a job to do.

A beard is a daily thing. Oils to soften it, balms to shape it, blades to line it, and a barber who actually knows the difference. This is the whole ritual, laid out plainly.

The Daily Ritual

Seven Steps That Change Everything

This is the exact routine our barbers walk clients through on their first beard appointment. Skip a step and you'll feel it in a week.

Jump to Step

01

Cleanse Daily

Beard wash — not shampoo. Shampoo strips the natural oils your beard needs. Use a dedicated beard cleanser 2–3 times a week.

Rinse with lukewarm water, never hot. Hot water dries out the follicles.
02

Condition 2–3×/week

A good beard conditioner softens the hair, reduces itch, and makes it easier to shape. Leave it in for 2–3 minutes before rinsing.

03

Dry & Comb Daily

Pat dry with a towel. Never rub. Comb in the direction of growth with a wide-tooth beard comb to detangle without breaking.

A wooden comb is better than plastic — less static, no snagging.
04

Oil Daily

3–5 drops of beard oil, worked into the skin underneath — not just the hair. This is the step most men skip, and it's the one that matters most.

05

Balm For longer beards

Balm is for shape and hold. If your beard is longer than two inches, balm keeps it looking intentional instead of wild.

06

Trim & Line Weekly

Clean up the cheek line and neckline once a week. Everything below the jawline should be trimmed, everything above left alone for shape.

Neckline: two fingers above the Adam's apple. That's the line.
07

Brush & Set Daily

Finish with a boar-bristle brush. It distributes oil evenly, trains the hair direction, and gives the beard a healthy shine.

Length Guide

How Long Is Your Beard, Really?

Beard length isn't measured in weeks — it's measured in millimetres, inches, and how far down your chest it sits. Here's what each stage actually looks like, and what it needs.

01 0–3mm

Stubble

2–5 days

Rough, even growth across the face. The "I forgot to shave" look, but on purpose. Skin still visible between hairs.

  • Daily wash with cold water
  • 2 drops of oil, worked into skin
  • Comb to train growth direction
02 3–8mm

Short Beard

1–2 weeks

The cheek line becomes visible, the neckline needs shaping, and the itch phase has just arrived. This is where most men quit.

  • Beard wash 2–3× per week
  • 3–4 drops of oil, daily
  • Trim cheek and neck line weekly
03 8–20mm

Medium Beard

3–6 weeks

Real shape starts forming. The chin pulls forward, the sides fill in, and a light balm helps it sit instead of sticking out.

  • Beard wash + conditioner weekly
  • 5 drops of oil, morning and night
  • Light balm for hold
04 20–50mm

Full Beard

2–3 months

The point where beards stop looking accidental. Needs real maintenance — regular trimming, proper oils, and a brush that earns its keep.

  • Wash + condition 2× per week
  • Oil + balm daily, both
  • Boar bristle brush, twice a day
  • Studio trim every 4–6 weeks
05 50mm+

Long Beard

6 months+

A commitment, not a look. Weekly trims, nightly oil, and a proper wooden comb become non-negotiable. Worth it if you mean it.

  • Full wash + condition routine
  • Oil, balm, and heat protection if blow-drying
  • Weekly edge trim at home
  • Studio reshape every 3–4 weeks

Habits

What Ruins a Beard

Five things that quietly wreck a good beard — and the fix for each.

Using Head Shampoo

Strips natural oils. Switch to beard wash 2–3 times a week, no more.

Towel-Rubbing Dry

Breaks hair and causes split ends. Pat dry, then comb while slightly damp.

Skipping Oil on Short Beards

Skin under the beard still needs moisture. Oil the skin, not just the hair.

Trimming Wet

Wet hair stretches, then dries shorter. You'll always cut too much. Trim dry.

Ignoring the Neckline

An unkempt neckline ages you 5 years. Two fingers above the Adam's apple is the line.
