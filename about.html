The Story

We didn't open a barbershop

We built a room where men can sit still and be taken seriously for forty-five minutes. – Marcus Hale, Founder

Chapter One

How We Got Here

2018
The First Chair

Iron & Oak started as a two-chair shop on the wrong end of Oak Street, in a space that used to sell secondhand records. Rent was cheap, the light was good, and the neighbor ran a coffee cart we used every single day.

Marcus had been cutting hair for eight years at that point — mostly in busy chain shops where the clock mattered more than the cut. He wanted something else. Slower. A room where the conversation could go where it needed to, where a man could say "I don't know what I want" and get an honest answer instead of a menu.

The first month, we did forty-two cuts. The second month, two hundred. By the end of that first year, we'd hired Elias — a beard specialist who'd been bouncing between shops looking for a home — and started sketching out what a proper studio would look like.

Today, Iron & Oak is four chairs, three master barbers, and a small team who actually like showing up. The coffee is still free. The conversation still goes where it needs to. And we still do every consultation before we pick up the scissors.

Our Manifesto

Five Things We Actually Believe

Not a brand statement. Just the stuff that shows up in how we work.

01

Talk First, Cut Second

Every service starts with a conversation. We ask about your hair type, your routine, and how much time you actually want to spend on it in the morning. A cut that looks great but takes twenty minutes to style isn't a good cut — it's a job.

02

Slow Is a Feature

We don't stack appointments. When you sit in our chair, the next forty-five minutes belong to you. No rushing, no glancing at the clock, no cutting corners because the next client is already in the doorway.

03

Honest Advice, Always

If a style won't work with your hair type, we'll tell you — kindly, but we'll tell you. If you don't need a product, we won't sell it to you. The relationship matters more than the receipt.

04

Small-Batch Everything

Every product we use is from a small maker we've met. Every oil, balm, and wash is chosen because it works on real hair — not because it has good margins or a nice label.

05

A Room, Not a Factory

We're not trying to open twelve locations. We're trying to make this one the best version of itself, and to still be cutting hair here when our current clients bring their sons in for their first proper cut.
