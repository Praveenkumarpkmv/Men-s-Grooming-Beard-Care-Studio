The Process

How a Cut Actually Happens

Six steps, forty-five minutes, no surprises. This is what you're paying for.

01

Consultation

We talk first. Hair type, lifestyle, how much time you want to spend styling — all of it matters before the first snip.

5 min
02

Wash & Prep

Clean hair cuts better. Warm wash, scalp massage, and a towel wrap while we set up.

8 min
03

The Cut

Scissor work, clipper work, or both. Built section by section so the shape lands exactly where it should.

20 min
04

Detailing

Edges, line-up, and neckline. This is where a good cut becomes a great one.

7 min
05

Hot Towel Finish

Hot towel on the neck, a touch of balm, and a quick once-over with the mirror.

3 min
06

Styling Tips

We show you how to recreate it at home. Products, amounts, direction — no gatekeeping.

2 min
